Welcome to Eclipse's AtCoder Collection!

This is a personally curated collection of my favourite problems on atcoder.jp, sorted in contest, then chronological order. Most of these aren't too difficult, and usually are older ARC/AGC cyans or blues as collated by this website (also useful for estimating problem difficulty, though of course what is easy/hard for most users may not be easy/hard for you. Also note that older problems are generally overrated in terms of difficulty when compared to newer ones). I'm definitely nowhere near the most skilled user on AtCoder, but these problems are enjoyable to me and I hope they will be to you too.

If you are looking for practice in competitive programming in general, *most* of these problems do not need in depth knowledge of specific topics (e.g. UFDS, segment trees), but focus more on adhoc problem solving. (That in itself contributes to my enjoyment of these problems)

This list is WIP. It will be updated as I do more problems, and as my skill level changes.
